1. Information We Collect

  • TikTok Account Information: When you authorize our service via official TikTok OAuth authentication, we receive authorized account identifiers (such as open_id, display name, and avatar) strictly necessary to verify user connection.
  • Content & Publishing Metadata: We process video assets, titles, captions, and publication schedules configured by you for automated upload.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To upload, schedule, and publish video content directly to your authorized TikTok account via official APIs.
  • To maintain secure authentication tokens and verify publication statuses.
  • To ensure system stability, detect posting bottlenecks, and prevent API rate-limit violations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or account information to any third parties or advertisers.

3. Data Storage & Security Standards

  • Token Security: OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored in encrypted, access-controlled environments.
  • Media Retention: Rendered video files and temporary assets are kept only as long as necessary to complete the scheduled upload and are purged periodically.

4. Third-Party Platform Integration (TikTok)

Our application interacts directly with TikTok via official TikTok Developer APIs. By connecting your account, you also acknowledge adherence to TikTok's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We do not access, view, or share private direct messages, contacts, or sensitive browsing activity.

5. User Data Control & Deletion Rights

  • Revoking Access: You can disconnect and revoke our application's access at any time directly through the TikTok mobile application (Settings and Privacy > Security & Permissions > Manage App Permissions).
  • Data Deletion Request: If you wish to request the immediate deletion of all stored tokens or publication logs associated with your account, please send an email request to our support contact below.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
